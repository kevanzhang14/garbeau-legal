Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

Garbeau ("the App") is a waste collection schedule app for municipalities in Quebec, Canada. This policy describes what data the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data

  • The App may request access to your device's GPS to help identify your municipality and collection zone.
  • Location coordinates are stored on your device and are used locally to determine your zone. Location access is optional — you can deny permission and select your city and zone manually.
  • When you use the address lookup feature, civic number, borough, and postal code may be transmitted to a municipal or third-party geocoding API (such as the City of Montréal or City of Laval open data endpoints) solely to resolve your collection zone. This data is not stored by us.
  • Platform geocoding and reverse-geocoding (via device OS services) may involve Apple or Google geocoding providers according to their own privacy policies.

App Preferences (Local)

  • Your selected city, zone, and general display settings are stored locally on your device using standard device storage.

Push Notification Data (Server-Side)

  • If you enable push notifications, the App obtains an Expo push token from your device and sends it — along with your selected city, zone, reminder lead times, muted collection types, and language preference — to our backend (hosted on Supabase).
  • This data is used exclusively to deliver collection-day reminders to your device. It is not used for marketing or profiling.
  • You can disable push notifications at any time in the App's settings, which stops future transmissions.

Crash Reports

  • If the App encounters an error in production, anonymous crash data (error message, stack trace, app version) may be sent to Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service.
  • Crash reports do not contain your location, city selection, personal information, or any data that can identify you.

2. Advertising

  • The App may display banner advertisements served by Google AdMob.
  • Ads are served as non-personalized by default. AdMob may collect and use the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) or equivalent platform identifier in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Pro subscribers (if applicable) may not see advertisements.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not:

  • Require or support user accounts, logins, or registration
  • Collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • Use analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs
  • Share or sell your personal data to third parties

4. Push Notifications

  • When push notifications are enabled, your Expo push token and notification preferences (city, zone, reminder hours, muted types, language) are stored on our Supabase backend to allow server-initiated reminders.
  • You can enable or disable push notifications at any time in the App's settings. Disabling notifications does not automatically delete existing server records; see Section 8 for deletion options.

5. Schedule Data

  • Collection schedules are bundled with the App. For select municipalities, the App may fetch updated schedule data from a public GitHub repository.
  • These requests are anonymous and do not transmit any user data.

6. Issue Reporting

  • The App includes a feature to report incorrect collection dates. This opens your device's email client with a pre-filled message.
  • You are in full control of whether to send the email and what information it contains.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — backend database for push notification tokens and preferences (Privacy Policy)
  • Expo Push Service — delivery of push notifications to your device (Privacy Policy)
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting (Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • City of Montréal / City of Laval open data APIs — address and zone resolution; requests are not stored by us
  • Apple / Google geocoding providers — platform-level geocoding via device OS, subject to Apple and Google privacy policies

8. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • Local preferences and cached data are stored on your device. You can clear them at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Server-side push notification records (token, preferences) are retained while you have notifications enabled. To request deletion of your server-side data, contact us at info@garbeau.ca.
  • Crash report data retained by Sentry is subject to Sentry's own retention policy.
  • RevenueCat transaction records are retained as required by Apple App Store / Google Play Store requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at info@garbeau.ca.
