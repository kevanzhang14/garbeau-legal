Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 9, 2026

Garbeau ("the App") is a waste collection schedule app for municipalities in Quebec, Canada. This policy describes what data the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data

  • The App may request access to your device's GPS to help identify your municipality and collection zone.
  • Location coordinates are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to any server.
  • Location access is optional. You can deny permission and select your city and zone manually.

App Preferences

  • Your selected city, zone, and notification settings are stored locally on your device using standard device storage.
  • This data is never transmitted to any server.

Crash Reports

  • If the App encounters an error in production, anonymous crash data (error message, stack trace, app version) may be sent to Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service.
  • Crash reports do not contain your location, city selection, personal information, or any data that can identify you.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not:

  • Require or support user accounts, logins, or registration
  • Collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers
  • Use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Transmit your location to any server
  • Share or sell any data to third parties

3. Local Notifications

  • The App can schedule local reminders for upcoming collection days.
  • Notifications are generated entirely on your device. No push notification tokens are sent to any server.
  • You can enable or disable notifications at any time in the App's settings.

4. Schedule Data

  • Collection schedules are bundled with the App. For select municipalities, the App may fetch updated schedule data from a public GitHub repository.
  • These requests are anonymous and do not transmit any user data.

5. Issue Reporting

  • The App includes a feature to report incorrect collection dates. This opens your device's email client with a pre-filled message.
  • You are in full control of whether to send the email and what information it contains.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • All user preferences and cached data are stored locally on your device.
  • You can clear all stored data at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at info@garbeau.ca.
